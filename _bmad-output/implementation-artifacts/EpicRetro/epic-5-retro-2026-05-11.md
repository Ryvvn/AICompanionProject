## 2. Successes & Strengths
- **Graceful Degradation:** The Screenpipe integration handles connection timeouts and failures smoothly, degrading to a foreground-app-only mode without crashing the core loop, adhering to the architecture.
- **Robust Accountability Engine:** The Banana Debt calculations and interval tracking work seamlessly to surface interventions exactly when needed.
- **Strict Tone Boundaries:** The `doomscrolling` persona effectively acts as a strict accountability partner without crossing the line into abusive or unhelpful language, successfully balancing the "Mean Banana" UX goal.

## 3. Challenges & Roadblocks
- **Screenpipe Complexity:** Extracting meaningful signals from Screenpipe without overloading the memory or exceeding token limits was challenging. A dual-path classification strategy was required to handle Screenpipe's heavy OCR payloads.
- **Testing Dependencies:** `pytest-mock` was missing from the `pyproject.toml` dependencies, causing an initial 68 test failures in CI. It was added as a dev dependency to resolve the issue.
